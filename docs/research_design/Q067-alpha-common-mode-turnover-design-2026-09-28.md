# Q067 — Alpha Common-Mode / Turnover Design Round

## Status

**DESIGN_ONLY_UNRANKED**

Q067 translates the completed Q066 diagnosis into two unranked follow-up hypotheses. It does not test them and does not establish a preferred family.

## Why these two questions

Q066 found three distinct features in the fixed Q041/Q045 evidence:

1. CONTROL drawdowns are persistent and multi-regime.
2. During CONTROL-underwater periods, return co-movement among different alpha families remains high even when their active-symbol overlap is modest.
3. Turnover/cost is a major failure mechanism for specific high-churn arms, while it is comparatively small for CONTROL.

The design therefore separates two different mechanisms rather than forcing one intervention to explain both.

## E1 — Alpha common-mode throttle

**Question:** Can exposure be reduced when the *alpha sleeves themselves* become highly co-moving?

This is deliberately different from Q030/RISK-C. RISK-C used cross-sectional dependence among underlying assets. E1 measures dependence among the return streams generated by the alpha sleeves.

The future fixed implementation must specify, before any coverage or performance evidence is inspected:

- sleeve universe and return construction,
- correlation lookback,
- activation threshold,
- exposure multiplier,
- recovery rule,
- observation/decision timing,
- missing-sleeve handling.

The result must remain interpretable as an exposure transformation, not a new predictive signal.

## E2 — Turnover hysteresis

**Question:** Can a deterministic rebalance deadband reduce avoidable turnover/cost drag in high-churn mechanisms without redefining the signal?

The future fixed implementation must specify:

- the minimum absolute or relative weight change,
- whether the threshold applies per asset or at portfolio level,
- carry-forward behavior,
- timing relative to signal formation,
- interaction with zero/entry/exit targets,
- cost accounting convention.

This mechanism is intended specifically for the cost-dominated failure pattern identified in Q066; it does not claim to solve the broader CONTROL drawdown problem by itself.

## Governance

Q067 does not rank E1 versus E2. Neither mechanism is performance-authorized by this document.

Each mechanism requires a complete parameter freeze, fresh symbol-disjoint coverage and PIT validation before any performance execution. The existing 13-gate contract remains unchanged.
