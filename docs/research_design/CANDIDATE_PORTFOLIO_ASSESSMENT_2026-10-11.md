## Portfolio policy

- Do not produce a numeric probability of strategy success from source readiness, a completed population, one pilot or a single replication. Probability remains **Nicht schätzbar**.
- Candidate ranking in this register is only a resource allocation decision. Do not use it to choose market-outcome windows, thresholds, assets, horizons or holdout.
- Source clock, immutable vintage, correction lineage, frozen entity mapping and future-row mutation invariance are prerequisites, not evidence of positive returns.
- Q224/Q228 receive design and source-feasibility preparation only; the current active focus and dispatch allow-list do not change.
- Q221 may be reconsidered for engineering only after an alternate immutable historical source provides transaction-level vintage evidence. A current archive listing or policy publication schedule is not enough.
- Keep paid APIs/services out of scope absent separate permission.
